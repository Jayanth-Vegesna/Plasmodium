Plasmodium falciparum Multi-Omics Portal

An interactive systems biology suite for exploring the genome, structural proteome, and drug-target interaction networks of the malaria parasite.

🧬
Genome Browser
Navigate the parasite's chromosomes, explore annotations, and extract FASTA sequences dynamically.
🔬
Proteome Explorer
Search and visualize AlphaFold 3D structures for thousands of P. falciparum proteins.
🕸️
Hemoglobin Interactome
Interactive physics network mapping the digestive vacuole, highlighting validated drug targets in gold.
